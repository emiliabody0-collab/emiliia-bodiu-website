Signal to habit

Health products only matter if people keep using them.

A launch tells you people were curious. The fourth month tells you whether the product found a place in their routine. That's what I build toward: onboarding people finish, programs that last past the first weeks, and features that make the next step easier.

Four calls, and what they changed

Each case starts with the result, then my role and the decision.

Case 1

The audience bet

Result
Active users grew from ~500 to ~10,000, and the 2025 scale-up went from $300K to $3M ARR in six months.
My role
I owned the roadmap across two mobile apps, the admin platform, and the CMS. Engineering, clinicians, marketing, and external partners built it with me.
The call
I tested four candidate audiences (PCOS, perimenopause, longevity, athletes) first on conversion and later on adherence. Then I made the bet on women with PCOS.

Case 2

The fourth month

Result
4th-month retention rose 2–2.5x.
My role
I owned the program redesign and led the in-app PCOS educational protocol with ObGyns, MDs, and medical writers.
The call
I replaced the 21-day program with long-term programs, supported by the PCOS protocol, the Aspect Scores glucose model, and coach operating procedures.

Case 3

Half the cost, and a setup people finish

Result
CGM unit cost halved, from $70 to $35. Onboarding completion rose to 80–90%.
My role
As Senior PM, I owned the CGM experience across 4 sensor models and the wearable layer (Vital, Apple Health, Google Health). Earlier, as Product Operations Manager, I rebuilt CGM onboarding end to end.
The call
I moved the CGM from prescription-only to over-the-counter. Before that, I replaced a stalled, semi-manual onboarding flow (prescription, doctor approval, fulfillment, coach intake) with one end-to-end process.

Case 4

AI where people were waiting

Result
An AI health coach for programs without a human coach cut response time and cost per user. Aida, an AI dietitian, orders groceries based on each user's health data.
My role
I launched Aida on the Instacart partner API, shipped the AI coach, and built the analytics foundation (Amplitude, BigQuery, Tableau) we used to measure product decisions.
The call
I gave coach-free programs an AI coach instead of no coach, and connected nutrition advice directly to grocery ordering.
